Most MVPs fail in one of two ways

Building the wrong product

Founders spend months building every feature they can imagine, launch, and find out nobody needed half of it. Lack of market need is one of the most common reasons startups shut down.

We start by cutting your idea down to the smallest version that proves people want it, and we write that down before any code.

The vibe-coding cliff

AI tools get a demo running in a weekend. Then real users arrive: logins break, payments fail silently, keys leak, and AI bills climb. The demo can't carry a business.

We keep what works in your prototype, treat it as the spec, and rebuild the parts that have to hold real users and real money.
